About

Stories are about people before they’re about brands.

I’m Eduardo Caceres, a filmmaker and communications professional with a background in advertising, documentary filmmaking and strategic communications.

Kin & Kith Creative was built around a simple idea: the most effective content starts with understanding people. I combine storytelling, strategy and production to create videos that don’t just look good—they have a purpose.

My work spans martial arts, healthcare, corporate communications and community storytelling. Whether the audience is a parent considering a jiu-jitsu academy, a patient choosing a clinic, or a team trying to explain a complex initiative, the starting point is always the same: understand who we’re speaking to and why the story should matter to them.
