Women’s Jiu-Jitsu

Women Who Train Together, Grow Together

Through Rosana Di Napoli’s perspective, this story explores what it feels like for a woman to step onto the mats and discover a place where she can grow stronger, more confident and more comfortable in herself.

At its heart, the story is about the friendships, trust and sense of belonging that develop through training together.

Member Story

Jiu-Jitsu Is for Everyone

Farsheed’s story is about taking the first step into something unfamiliar and discovering a community along the way.

Through his experience on the mats, the video explores how jiu-jitsu can become more than training—it can build confidence, create friendships and become an important part of everyday life.

Family Story

A Family on the Mats

For this family, jiu-jitsu becomes something they can experience together.

The story follows the connection created through shared training—the encouragement before class, the challenges on the mats and the moments afterward—and shows how a martial art can become part of a family’s routine, memories and relationships.

This story looks beyond the techniques taught during the seminar and focuses on what happens when a community comes together to learn.

Through Jonathan’s perspective, moments from the mats and the energy of the room, the video captures the curiosity, challenge and connection that made the experience memorable.
