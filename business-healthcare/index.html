Healthcare & Community Story

Paul Myers Tower — Welcome Figures

A story about the journey of creating the welcome figures for the new Paul Myers Tower at Lions Gate Hospital.

Through the voices of the carvers, the film explores the meaning carried in the figures, the care and intention behind their creation, and what they hope these works will communicate to patients, families, staff and the wider community. The story connects craftsmanship, place and the idea of welcome—showing how art can help shape the feeling of a healthcare space before someone even walks through the doors.

StoryCraftsmanship, meaning, place and community
FormatInterview-led documentary storytelling
ApproachCarver perspectives, process imagery and visual detail

Recruitment: VGH Medical Imaging

Emma’s story follows a career journey that begins in Ireland and leads to Vancouver, where she joins the medical imaging team at Vancouver General Hospital.

Through her perspective, the video turns recruitment into a personal story about making a major move, finding a new professional home and building a career in healthcare. Follow Jamie, a Perioperative Nurse in Sechelt

Jamie’s story offers a look at what it means to build both a career and a life in Sechelt.

Following Jamie through her experience as a perioperative nurse, the video blends the professional side of working at Sechelt Hospital with the lifestyle and community that surround it. The goal is to help prospective nurses imagine not only the role itself, but what living and working on the Sunshine Coast can actually feel like.
